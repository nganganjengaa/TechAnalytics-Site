About Us

The Department of Technology & Analytics was established to prepare students for careers in software engineering, data analytics, networking, and cyber security. We combine theoretical foundations with hands-on laboratory work, giving students practical experience with real tools used across the industry.

Our staff work closely with students to build skills in programming, data-driven decision making, and modern IT infrastructure, preparing graduates for both employment and further study.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality education and training in technology and analytics, fostering innovation and excellence in the field.

Our Vision

To be a leading department recognized for excellence in technology and analytics education, research, and community engagement.

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Innovation, and Collaboration.
